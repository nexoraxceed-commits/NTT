Sign-up and password-reset codes are emailed by NTT through Mailjet. Send yourself a test to confirm it works.

Register a user

New users begin as PENDING with no Auth UID. They complete activation through signup or their first authenticated sign-in.

Registered users

Managed users can be SENIOR, JUNIOR, or REDUNDANT_USER. Redundant users have view-only access.
