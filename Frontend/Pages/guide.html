①Getting startedSign up, sign in and install the app
  1. Sign up with your college emailUse your @astra.xlri.ac.in email on the Sign up page. Only emails added by an admin can sign up; ask a senior if yours is refused.
  2. Complete your welcomeThe first time you sign in you'll see a short welcome. Add your roll number at the end.
  3. Install it on your phoneiPhone: open NTT in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome → ⋮ menu → Install app.
  4. Turn on notificationsAllow notifications when asked, so you hear about reviews, mentions and new tasks straight away.
②Your dashboardWhat the cards mean
  1. My work and TeamSwitch between your own tasks and everyone's. My work counts tasks you own or review.
  2. Status cardsTo do, In progress, Overdue and Done. Tap a card to see the tasks behind the number.
  3. Needs your attentionReviews waiting for you, @mentions, and activity on tasks you watch.
  4. The orbGreen means nothing is overdue, amber means something is due this week, red means something is late.
③Working on a taskFrom start to done
  1. Start workOpen the task and tap Start work so the team can see it's moving.
  2. Log hoursUnder Hours, tap a quick pick (15m, 30m, 1h…) or use − / +, add one line on what you did, then Log hours. Log on the day you work.
  3. Share progressPost an update in Work and updates. Paste a link to your doc or deck so your reviewer can open it.
  4. Send for reviewWhen it's ready, tap Send for review. Your reviewer approves it or sends it back with a note.
  5. If it comes backRead the note in Activity, fix it, and send it for review again.
  6. On your phoneTap the blue + button at the bottom right for the next step, logging hours or posting an update.
④Notifications and mentionsGetting someone's attention
  1. Mention someoneType @ in an update and pick a name. They get a notification that links straight to the task.
  2. The bellThe number on the bell is what you haven't read yet. It rings when something new arrives.
  3. Deleting your updatePress and hold Delete on your own update for a second. A quick tap won't delete anything.
⑤People and the leaderboardWho's who and how effort is counted
  1. People pageEveryone, grouped into the senior team and juniors, with hours and finished tasks for the last 30 days. Change the dates to look at any period.
  2. LeaderboardOn the dashboard. 1 point per hour logged, 1 per task finished, +0.5 if it was on time. Filter to Seniors or Juniors.
★For the senior teamProjects, assigning work and reviews
  1. Create a projectProjects → +. Add the team (juniors doing the work), supervisors (seniors who review) and optional watchers.
  2. Create and assign tasksInside a project, add a task with an owner, a reviewer and a due date. Anyone on the project team can add tasks too.
  3. Review workTasks sent to you appear under Needs your attention. Open one, check the linked work, then Approve and close or Send back with a note.
  4. Keep juniors movingThe Needs a check-in list on the dashboard (seniors only) shows who hasn't logged hours recently.

Quick answers

The app says it's "waking up the server".
The server sleeps when nobody has used it for a while. The first load can take up to a minute; after that it's quick.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in page tap Forgot? and follow the email.
My roll number is wrong.
Open the welcome again from here and update your profile on the last step.
